溪岸创立于成都，以自然为源。在马来西亚，由 RICCIONE REKA 落地 —— 一个 2015 年创立的实木品牌，十年只做一件事：把木头做成能陪人过日子的东西。 Sail was founded in Chengdu, with nature as its source. In Malaysia it is brought to ground by RICCIONE REKA — a solid-wood brand founded in 2015, ten years spent on one thing: turning timber into pieces people can live with.
