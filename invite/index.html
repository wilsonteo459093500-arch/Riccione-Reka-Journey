关于我们About us

在溪岸，
回归生活的美好 In the Sail,
return to the beauty of life

溪岸 Sail 创立于成都，承载着对理想生活的追求 —— 以自然为源，让设计回归宁静与美好。我们相信，家的温度，能在时光中沉淀。 Founded in Chengdu, Sail embodies the pursuit of an ideal life. With nature as our source, we bring design back to serenity. We believe the warmth of a home deepens over time.

在马来西亚，溪岸由 RICCIONE REKA 以东南亚总代理的身份落地。RICCIONE 是一个 2015 年创立的马来西亚实木家具品牌 —— 十年只做一件事：把木头做成能陪着人过日子的东西。 In Malaysia, Sail is brought to ground by RICCIONE REKA as the exclusive Southeast Asia distributor. RICCIONE is a Malaysian solid-wood furniture brand founded in 2015 — ten years spent on one thing: turning timber into pieces people can actually live with.
